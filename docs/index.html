Dispatches

15 Nov2026

Free Play opens, the maps wake up, and new music

Free Play is in: 20 standalone maps from Pushover to Carnage, each playable with any race that can actually solve it. Many more are on the way.

  • Maps are getting livelier: critters lurk on ledges, wildcard beasts drop in, and the new biomes (frost, dunes, giant forest, vortex lands, shattered borders and temple ruins) each play by their own rules.
  • New music tracks for the march. Pick one, or let them rotate, in Settings.
  • A new website, laid out as the wandering scholar's journals: lore, a page and field study for every race, a world globe, community map links and a feedback form for testers.
