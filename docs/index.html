Marching Orders

Tiny troops. Big trouble.

Lead a marching rabble of goblins, frogs, imps and worse. Dig, build and blow your way home, or sabotage a friend.

PLAY NOWMAP BUILDER

Play offline: install Rabblings to your desktop or home screen.

Pick your rabble

Eight races, two clans each, and every one plays differently. Take a look.

    Small soldiers. Bad plans.

    Guide the march

    Your troops walk on their own. Hand out skills at the right moment and get enough of them home.

    2 player sabotage

    Race a friend on one screen. Steal their troops, wreck their route and enjoy the shouting.

    Swords and spells

    Arm your rabble, clear nests and golems, and save rare magic for when it counts. Campaign mana is gone once spent.

    Grumpy little morale

    Lose too many and your troops answer back, refuse orders or sit down in protest.

    Build your own maps

    Paint terrain or generate a starting point, add traps and triggers, then share the code.

    Your NeoLemmix levels

    Got NeoLemmix? Bring your level packs into Rabblings and replay them with our races. Import NeoLemmix levels.

    Classic mode

    Want the pure old-school puzzle? Switch off the morale and the chatter.
