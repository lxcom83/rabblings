What we store, and why

You can play Rabblings and build maps without an account. Your saved maps stay in your own browser unless you share them.

If you make an account

  • Your email address, so we can send you sign-in links. It is never shown to other players.
  • Your player name, if you choose one. It is public, and shown in Rabblings games and on maps you build.
  • When your account was made and when you last changed your name.

That is all. There are no ads, we do not track you around the web, and we never sell or share your details.

Where it is kept

Accounts are run by Supabase, on their servers in the United States. They also send the sign-in emails. Your browser remembers that you are signed in until you sign out.

Deleting your account

Open Sign in on the home page, then choose Delete my account. Your email, player name and anything linked to your account are removed straight away. Maps saved in your browser are not affected.
