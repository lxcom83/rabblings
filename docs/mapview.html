Maps

One click opens 13 sample maps with their routes, so you can look around, edit and try every tool. Your own files and cloud folders are never touched.

Add your game files

Load the base game first, then your mod, so your files win. You can add a .zip or the OXCE .apk directly, plain files, or a whole folder. Files stay on this device.

Loaded

Where to get the files

Base game: the UFO folder (TERRAIN, MAPS, ROUTES, GEODATA/PALETTES.DAT), or the OXCE .apk, which carries it. Your mod: its TERRAIN, MAPS, ROUTES folders and the Terrain ruleset (.rul). The game's art is not stored on this site: each person loads their own copy from Drive, Dropbox or their device.

My locations

Auto archive

Each save is also copied, with a date and time in the name, into the Archive location you choose. Only your edited MAP, RMP and MCD files are copied, into your own Dropbox or Drive. Nothing is stored on the Rabblings server except the folder links and a log of what was archived.
