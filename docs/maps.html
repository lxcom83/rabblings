Share a map or pack

Sign in to share a link. Anyone can browse without an account.

What are you sharing?

Packs on GitHub, Gist, Cloudflare R2 or Supabase Storage open straight in the game. Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive work too, but players must download the file and use OPEN FILE.

How sharing works

  1. In the game, open Map Builder → My Maps, then save your pack or map file.
  2. Upload it to your own storage and copy a public link.
  3. Paste the link here with a title and a few words.

Be careful what you openOnly open packs from people you trust. The game checks every map before saving it, and shows you where a pack comes from before importing.
