How to play

  • Raise / Lower: tap a corner of the land (hold to repeat). Flat land lets your followers build homes, and homes grow with your numbers.
  • Flick: tap a follower or a home. Your own lot work twice as hard (2 mana, and Dread rises); a rival loses one (6 mana).
  • Oi, you: tap a pale relic and the nearest follower fetches it.
  • Champion: once per mission, raise one follower into a strand champion (40 mana).
  • Weaves: Upheave, Flood, Rot and Gloom work only within reach of your shrines (the dotted ring). Warded ground blocks them; dark bedrock, old paving and deep water can never be changed.
  • Shrines grow through five stages as homes gather around them, and pay more mana as they grow. Take every rival shrine to win.
  • Between missions the Shaping altar breeds one new trait into your people. Every gift has a price, some paid later.
