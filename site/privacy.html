What we store, and why

You can play Rabblings and build maps without an account. Your saved maps, progress, scores and Free Play notes stay in your own browser unless you choose to send them.

If you make an account

  • Your email address, so we can send you sign-in links. It is never shown to other players.
  • Your player name, if you choose one. It is public, and shown in Rabblings games, on scores you post and on maps you share.
  • When your account was made and when you last changed your name.
  • Whether attempt logging is on for you. Only you can see this setting.

Free Play attempt logs

When you play a Free Play map with a race that has not been confirmed for that map, the game notes: the map, the race and clan, the difficulty, classic mode on or off, whether you passed, how many troops you saved out of how many were needed, how long it took, and when. Nothing else, and no details about your device.

Logging is off unless you switch it on. If you are signed in and have switched on Log my Free Play attempts on your account page, these notes are sent to your account when you visit your account page (and, in a later update, straight from the game). They help the designer learn which races can really beat each map. Only you can see your own logs; the designer sees totals per map and race.

To stop logging again: open your account page and switch off Log my Free Play attempts. Nothing more is sent. You can also delete every attempt you have sent with Clear my logged attempts.

Things you choose to share

  • High scores you post are public, with your player name.
  • Community map listings are public: the link, title, description and designer credit you enter. The map file stays wherever you keep it; we never copy it.
  • Thumbs up: the total on each map is public. Which maps you voted for is visible only to you.
  • Feedback you send is read only by the Rabblings team, with your player name. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent: the form gives you text to copy and send yourself.

Where it is kept

Accounts and everything above are kept by Supabase, on their servers in the United States. They also send the sign-in emails. Your browser remembers that you are signed in until you sign out. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and the globe page loads three.js from cdnjs; those services see your connection's address, as any website you visit does.

There are no ads, we do not track you around the web, and we never sell or share your details.

Deleting everything

Open your account page and choose Delete everything. Your email, player name, attempt logs, posted scores, shared map listings, votes and feedback are all removed straight away. Maps and progress saved in your browser are not affected; clear your browser's site data for rabblings.com to remove those too.
