Thy Will Be Mud

You are a petty demigod in an older age, when the demigods first fought over the scattered, freshly mutated survivors. Raise the bog, flatten the hills, and let your followers spread. Then breed them into something that can win.

Play the demoHow it plays

Early demo, art still arriving. Plays in your browser on phone, tablet, mouse and keyboard, or a controller. Progress saves on your device.

How it plays

Shape the land. Shape the people.

Your followers do the rest themselves: they settle flat ground, breed, gather, haul and fight without orders. You steer them only by changing the world around them, and by the occasional flick.

Raise and lower the land

Flat ground lets your people build. Their homes grow from lean-tos to hill holds as your whole population rises. Water is the great barrier: bridge it to attack, sink land to cut a rival off.

The flick

One divine fingertip. A flicked follower works twice as hard, muttering, and your Dread rises. Flick too often and the village sits down on strike.

The Shaping altar

Between missions, spend what your people gathered to breed traits into the strain. Every gift has a price that comes due later. Your people's name changes as they do.

Shrines

Shrines are the lenses your magic passes through. Take your rivals' shrines to win; lose all of yours and your demigod is gone.

Concept art

From hovel to hill hold

Five home sizes, chosen by how much flat ground a settlement has and how many followers you have across the whole map. The land changes them too: stilts over bog, homes dug into hillsides.

Your people

An older people, not yet anything you know

The early peoples only hint at who they might become. Breed in long arms, hard scales or an ember under the skin, and see what survives. The scholar has guesses. He never confirms them.

Magic

Five strands, woven through shrines

No fireballs, no lightning bolts. Mud Age shrine-keepers held that every power is a weave of the five strands. You cast within reach of a shrine you hold. Weave outside what your people are used to and it may snap back.

Heat and ColdFloods, frost and scorched roofs.
ForceShoves, upheavals, a follower sent flying.
EarthHills from bog, cracks through fields.
Life and DecayPlagues that spread from hut to hut.
Shadow and LightGloom that swallows a village whole.

In the demo

New lands, and ground that fights back

Each mission moves to new country. The demo crosses the Mire, the Rime Flats and the Bake; the Twisted Lands and the Old Stones follow in the full game. Homes are built to suit the land, and some ground no god can move.

Works that matter

Homes grow mines, fields, beast pens, substance works, ruin digs, burial grounds, war camps and watchtowers, each picked by the land around it and each doing a job.

Traps

Watchtowers and war camps lay pits, snares, stakes, log traps and blast mounds. Yours you can see. Theirs you find the hard way.

The flick

Manifesting the hand costs mana: a little to hurry your own, more to knock a rival flat. Flick your own too often and Dread rises.

Now and next

Up to four demigods, and maps you make

A three-mission campaign against one to three computer demigods, on small, medium or large maps, with the Shaping altar between missions.

Ground no god can move

Bedrock, ancient paving, deep water and warded ground make fixed cliffs, moats and chokepoints.
